About GLOBAL FUSION

Trade connections
built on trust.

We are building an India-based export sourcing and commission-based business focused on connecting international buyers with suitable Indian suppliers.

Who we are

A practical link between buyers and makers.

GLOBAL FUSION is designed to make the first steps of international sourcing more approachable. We listen to buyer requirements, identify potential supplier options and facilitate business communication.

Our role and responsibilities for each transaction are agreed with the relevant parties in advance. We do not claim to be the manufacturer, freight forwarder, customs broker or certifying authority unless separately and expressly engaged in that capacity.

Business model

Commission-based sourcing facilitation.

Depending on the agreed arrangement, GLOBAL FUSION may earn a sourcing or introduction commission when a transaction is successfully concluded. Commission, payment trigger, scope and responsibilities must be documented with the relevant party before work begins.

GLOBAL FUSION is an emerging business. Company registrations, export authorisations, certifications, track record and market coverage should only be represented where verified and applicable. This website intentionally avoids unverified claims.
